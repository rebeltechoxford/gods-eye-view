# Device-stream foundation

This is a server-only foundation for the presence-authorized routing design in `TAK_DIRECTED_CHAT_DESIGN.md`. It does not enable directed chat or claim native-client interoperability. The existing gateway is unchanged until its separate opt-in integration is accepted.

`server/providers/tak/deviceStreams.js` wraps the existing verified-TLS server-link factory. It maintains one shared feed for SA-off devices and one stream per explicitly publishing device UID. The first endpoint-bearing event is that device's authorized self-SA; no registration control or position is invented. A presence lease lasts at most 30 seconds, refreshed only by valid self-SA. Stop, lost authorization, last-reference removal, expiry and destruction close the corresponding route and cancel unwritten work. Duplicate UID tabs share one stream and a single presence publisher.

Limits include 4 upstreams per pool (including the shared feed), 32 process-wide, 32 identities per pool, 256 pending submissions / 1 MiB per principal and 8 MiB process-wide queued bytes. Process-wide UID claims reject concurrent cross-pool identity reuse. Verified TLS, existing server-link queue expiry/write-buffer limits and actual submission outcome semantics remain authoritative. A submitted write is not peer receipt or confirmed registration.

Every wire has a 200 events/s, 1,000 burst frame cap and 1 MiB/s byte cap. Delivery has a fixed 4 MiB/s principal budget divided fairly among its live device UIDs. It does **not** spend one shared 200 events/s bucket for every copied public feed. Four 100 Hz copies can reach four intended devices without one recipient consuming another's frame budget; a byte flood on one wire cannot consume another device's reserved delivery share.

Ingress carries its actual stream owner through per-device deduplication and contact replay. A missing/stripped `marti` destination never authorizes principal-wide fanout. Device streams feed only that UID's authorized tabs; the shared feed reaches SA-off devices only. Replay and dedup maps are partitioned under fixed aggregate bounds, private chat is not contact replay, and retired-stream callbacks cannot reach a new owner. The gateway's later adapter must retain its existing event authorization and broadcast-only chat policy.

Focused tests in `src/tooling/takDeviceStreams.test.mjs` cover lazy SA-off operation, real USER-SA construction, exact identity/endpoint/freshness, duplicate-tab ownership, expiry/stop, 4 per principal and 32 per process limits, cross-pool UID conflict/release, stripped-routing isolation/replay, copied-feed fairness, byte flooding, aggregate queued work, cancellation/reconnect epochs, late outcomes and receive-only behavior. These are injected transport tests; real TLS/server and browser acceptance follow integration review.
